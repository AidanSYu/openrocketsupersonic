# Cmq Second-Source Closure: Bhagwandin & Sahu 2013 (ARL-TR-6725)

**Author:** Aidan Yu
**Date:** 2026-05-02
**Branch:** `cmq-bhagwandin-second-source`

## Citation

Bhagwandin, V.A. and Sahu, J. (2013). *Numerical Prediction of Pitch
Damping Stability Derivatives for Finned Projectiles.* ARL-TR-6725,
US Army Research Laboratory, Aberdeen Proving Ground, MD, November 2013.
DTIC Accession: ADA592550. Distribution Statement A — public release,
approved for unlimited distribution.

## Why this source closes the second-source gap

The open AST-reviewer audit item required a second independent Cmq dataset on
a non-Basic-Finner geometry. The previous AST submission rested on
ADA636861 / Tobak / Dupuis, all of which test the **Army-Navy Basic Finner
(ANF)**: a 10-deg cone (2.84 calibers) followed by a 7.16-cal cylinder, with
four 1x1 cal rectangular fins. The Albisser (2015) thesis was rejected as a
second source because it tested the same ANF geometry.

ARL-TR-6725 reports CFD pitch-damping predictions for **two** geometries:

| | ANF | AFF (Air Force Modified Finner) |
|---|---|---|
| Nose | 10-deg sharp cone, 2.84 cal | tangent ogive, 2.5 cal |
| Body | 7.16 cal cylinder | 7.5 cal cylinder |
| Total L/D | 10.0 | 10.0 |
| Fin planform | 1x1 cal rectangular (square) | clipped-delta, sharp LE/TE |
| Fin LE | sharp | sharp |
| Diameter D | 30 mm (1 caliber) | 30 mm (1 caliber) |
| CG location | 5.50 cal from nose | 4.80 cal from nose |
| Mass | (see ADA636861) | 0.6643 kg |
| Inertias | (see ADA636861) | Ix = 7.197e-4 kg-m^2, Iy = 4.857e-3 kg-m^2 |

AFF is geometrically distinct from ANF in two of the three top-level shape
descriptors: **nose family** (curved tangent ogive vs straight cone) and
**fin planform** (delta vs rectangular). This is sufficient to count as a
non-Basic-Finner second source for the audit.

## Methodology

Bhagwandin & Sahu's CFD predictions use two independent dynamic methods:

- **Planar pitching** — forced sinusoidal pitch oscillation at amplitude
  A = 0.25 deg and reduced frequency k = 0.1, time-accurate URANS in CFD++.
- **Lunar coning** — coning motion at half-angle sigma = 0.5 deg and
  nondimensional rate Omega = 0.0025, with Magnus correction.

Both methods report the combined pitch damping moment coefficient
**PDM = (Cmq + Cm_alpha-dot)** and pitch damping force coefficient
**PDF = (CNq + CN_alpha-dot)**.

For comparison against OpenRocket Plus (ORP), planar pitching is the
appropriate physical motion: it is what `BarrowmanStabilityCalculator.calculateDampingMoments`
strip-theory + transonic Gaussian augmentation actually models. Lunar coning
introduces Magnus and roll/pitch coupling that the ORP path does not
represent. Lunar coning predictions are retained in the source CSV for
completeness but are not used in the headline comparator.

## Quantitative results (ORP combined PDM vs Bhagwandin CFD planar pitching)

Comparator artifacts:

- `paper/data/csv/bhagwandin_aff_cmq_comparator_2026_05_02.csv`
- `paper/data/csv/bhagwandin_anf_cmq_comparator_2026_05_02.csv`
- `paper/data/csv/bhagwandin_sahu_2013_anf_aff_cmq.csv` (digitized source)

Both comparators were generated by the new test class
`core/src/test/java/info/openrocket/core/aerodynamics/BhagwandinSahuCmqComparatorTest.java`.
The test passed on Windows, JDK 17.0.18, Gradle 9.2.0 with the AFF fin
planform calibrated to ARL-TR-6725 Figure 3 (root chord 1.33 cal, tip
chord 0.66 cal, LE sweep 53.0&deg; / 0.67 cal, exposed semispan 0.50
cal, thickness 0.047 cal, hexagonal section):

```
[AFF] MAPE = 62.97 %  (worst 151.88 % at M=1.000, 13/13 points)
[ANF] MAPE = 50.78 %  (worst 192.89 % at M=0.930, 13/13 points)
```

The headline numbers are dominated by the transonic band (M = 0.93 - 1.10),
where ORP's `k_transonic = 1 + 2.5 * exp(-((M-1)/0.15)^2)` Gaussian augmentation
in `BarrowmanStabilityCalculator` overshoots the CFD baseline by a factor of
~1.5-2.5x on AFF (smaller than the 3-6x seen with the placeholder fins,
because the corrected planform produces a smaller absolute |Cmq| that
still gets multiplied by the same transonic factor). This is a previously
known finding (see `BasicFinnerCmqBenchmarkTest` class Javadoc, "Per-band
breakdown") that this source independently confirms on a second geometry.

The supersonic regime (M >= 1.29 for ANF, M >= 1.30 for AFF), where Bhagwandin
& Sahu's Section 4.2.1 explicitly shows the CFD itself converges with the
Dupuis 2002 and West 1981 free-flight experimental scatter, is the regime
where ORP physics validity should be judged:

| Geometry | Mach band | Points | MAPE | Worst |delta_pct| |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AFF | 1.30 - 2.50 | 5 | **36.25 %** | 44.47 % at M=2.50 |
| ANF | 1.29 - 4.50 | 8 | **28.02 %** | 33.82 % at M=2.00 |

Per-point supersonic delta_pct values (from the comparator CSVs):

- **AFF supersonic:** -28.86 %, -26.87 %, -39.00 %, -42.05 %, -44.47 %
  (M = 1.30, 1.50, 1.75, 2.00, 2.50). All five points underpredict |damping|;
  sign pattern now matches ANF.
- **ANF supersonic:** -20.87, -31.48, -33.82, -33.29, -31.22, -28.71, -24.17, -20.62 %
  (M = 1.29, 1.50, 2.00, 2.50, 3.00, 3.50, 4.00, 4.50). Consistent with the
  -20 to -50 % "supersonic underprediction" band already documented against
  ADA636861 free-flight.

With the corrected planform, the AFF bias is unambiguously in the **same
direction** as ANF (ORP underpredicts |damping|, all 5 points negative),
which strengthens the conclusion that the supersonic underprediction is a
model-physics issue rather than a geometry-specific artifact. The
placeholder-era M=1.30 outlier (+4.79 %) was a geometry-error effect that
disappeared once the planform was calibrated.

## Verdict

**B-level / strong methodology cross-check, not closure-grade.** Justification:

- **Pro A-level:** AFF supersonic delta_pct sign matches ANF on all five
  supersonic points (was 4/5 with the placeholder), confirming the
  underprediction is a physics-level finding reproducible across geometries.
  Geometry is independent of the existing ANF source family (tangent ogive
  vs. cone, clipped-delta vs. square fins). Bhagwandin & Sahu's own
  Section 4.2.1 confirms their CFD itself converges with the Dupuis 2002
  free-flight band in the same regime, so disagreement against the CFD is
  effectively disagreement against experiment.
- **Against A-level:** AFF supersonic MAPE = 36.25 % over 5 points sits
  just above the AST roadmap's 30 % closure threshold for the supersonic
  regime, with the worst point (M=2.50, -44.47 %) right at the upper edge
  of the previously documented -20 to -50 % "supersonic underprediction"
  band. The 5-point sample is also limited to M <= 2.50, so the dataset
  does not span the full M < 4.5 envelope the paper claims. The
  full-envelope 13-point MAPE of 62.97 % is dominated by the transonic
  Gaussian augmentation issue, which is a known A-level finding the AST
  paper already acknowledges, not a Bhagwandin-specific defect, but it
  prevents the dataset from being interpreted as "ORP is closed-form
  validated against AFF across all Mach".
- **Net:** Use Bhagwandin & Sahu 2013 as a **second-source cross-check** in
  the AST paper, citing the supersonic-band MAPE numbers and explicitly
  stating that (a) the geometry independence requirement is satisfied,
  (b) the ORP supersonic underprediction trend is reproduced on a different
  geometry with consistent sign on every supersonic point, and (c) the
  transonic discrepancy is a separately documented finding. Promotion to
  A-level would require either widening the AFF Mach envelope or
  reducing the supersonic MAPE below 30 % through a model-physics fix
  (the underprediction is currently a known open finding, not specific to
  this dataset).

**Status: D / not a validation source (resolved 2026-05-03).** With
WebFetch re-enabled, the HISA notes page was successfully retrieved on
2026-05-03. The page text contributes the methodology summary, a
geometry topology ("2.5 caliber tangent-ogive cylinder forebody with 4
trapezoidal fins"), Mach band 0.6–2.5, and a reference list. Crucially,
the page validates against **Bhagwandin & Sahu (2013) ARL-TR-6725**
(the very ADA592550 dataset already digitized in this commit) and
DRDC SF/MF free flights (the Dupuis 2002 family already used as our
ANF anchor). The HiSA case therefore shares experimental anchors with
our existing sources and adds no independent third source. The
numerical Cmq curves on the page are embedded in two PNG plots
(`aeroNormSlope.png`, `aeroPitchSlope.png`) which a text-mode WebFetch
cannot decode; even after digitization those values would not be
independent. **Do not cite HISA as a Cmq validation source in the AST
manuscript.** Disposition recorded in
`paper/data/md/hisa_modified_finner_summary.md`.

### AFF fin planform — resolved (2026-05-03)

The placeholder fin planform in `makeAirForceModifiedFinner` was
calibrated against ARL-TR-6725 Figure 3 (page 9) using the values
verbatim from the figure callouts:

| Quantity | Verbatim Figure 3 value | Fixture value |
|---|---|---|
| Root chord (Cr) | 1.33 cal | 1.33 cal |
| Tip chord (Ct) | derived from sweep | 0.66 cal |
| LE sweep angle | 53.0&deg; | implicit (sweep length 0.67) |
| LE sweep length | 0.67 cal | 0.67 cal |
| Total tip-to-tip span | 2.00 cal | (implies 0.50 cal exposed) |
| Exposed semispan | (2.00 - 1.00) / 2 = 0.50 cal | 0.50 cal |
| Thickness | 0.0470 cal | 0.047 cal |
| Cross-section | hexagonal w/ 0.25 cal LE/TE bevels, R 0.004 cal | HEXAGONAL |
| 4 fins, sharp LE/TE | yes | yes |

Geometric self-check: tan(53.0&deg;) &times; 0.50 cal = 0.664, matches
the 0.67 cal LE-sweep-length callout to <1 %.

Tip chord was derived because Figure 3 calls out the sweep length but
not Ct directly; Ct = Cr - LE_sweep_length = 1.33 - 0.67 = 0.66 cal,
which is consistent with a clipped-delta fin whose trailing edge is
perpendicular to the body axis (the figure confirms this topology).

**Comparator impact.** With the corrected planform, the AFF
full-envelope MAPE dropped from 223.48 % to **62.97 %** (worst point
151.88 % at M=1.000, was 570.80 %). The supersonic-band (M >= 1.30,
5 points) MAPE rose from 18.96 % to **36.25 %** because the corrected
(smaller) fins produce less absolute |Cmq|, sharpening the previously
documented ORP supersonic underprediction; the sign of all five
supersonic points is now negative, matching ANF cleanly. The Verdict
section above has been updated with the new numbers and rationale.

The placeholder-era M=1.30 outlier (+4.79 %) was a geometry-error
artifact and has correctly disappeared.
