The Rocket Flight Database is an externally curated corpus of instrumented
sounding-rocket and high-power-rocketry flights assembled to benchmark
supersonic trajectory simulators. The headline table comprises 25 flights
spanning Mach 0.54--4.33, each pairing a measured apogee (barometric, GPS,
optical-track, or integrated-accelerometer ground truth) with two predictions:
the publicly recorded RASAero~II \cite{rogers2015} altitude and an OpenRocket-Plus
\cite{niskanen2009} re-simulation. A second table reports approximately 20
exploratory high-Mach historical sounding-rocket flights (to Mach~7.2) reproduced
in full, without outcome-based selection. Each record carries per-flight
provenance to the originating source document. The data are released CC-BY-4.0 at
Zenodo (DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.20531977}). Their reuse value is as an open,
non-circular benchmark for validating rocketry trajectory and aerodynamics codes
\cite{rocketpy2021}; the underlying accuracy analysis is reported in the companion
research article \cite{yu2026jsr}.
